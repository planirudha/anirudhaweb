04 — Lessons

Things experience taught me.

01

Ship, then sharpen.

Real feedback beats perfect plans. The fastest way to the right answer is putting something real in front of people.

02

Systems fail; people recover them.

Great tools don't replace teams — they give them leverage when it matters most.

03

Clarity is a kindness.

Writing things down, saying what you mean, and making decisions visible saves everyone time.

04

Stay a beginner.

Every chapter of my career started with not knowing enough. That's usually where the best work begins.
